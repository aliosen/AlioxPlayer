Private Top 10 Stats is optional. If you enable it, the app counts completed plays locally to show your top 10 songs and albums inside the app. The stats feature does not send these counts to a server. You may choose to include them in a manual backup as described below.

Manual backups are optional and user-initiated. You choose which categories to include, such as liked songs, playlists, and Private Top 10 Stats, and you choose where the backup file is saved. You can remove local app data by disconnecting accounts, deleting downloads, resetting Private Top 10 Stats in the app settings, deleting manual backup files from the folder where you saved them, clearing app data in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove local playlists, settings, downloaded files, and Private Top 10 Stats stored only on the device.
